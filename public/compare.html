Side by side.

Great for teams. Not built to govern a program.

What they do well

Flexible boards and timelines, fast team adoption, automations and solid built-in AI for individual teams' work.

Where programs strain

Every team builds its own board, so portfolio health, RAID and decisions become custom views someone maintains. Executive reporting still ends up in hand-built slides.

How HorizonView fits

Teams can stay in monday or Asana. HorizonView rolls their status into one governed portfolio, scores health with the reasons behind it, writes the summary and builds the SteerCo deck.

Powerful grids. A lot of sheets to keep in sync.

What they do well

A familiar spreadsheet feel with Gantt, forms, dashboards and strong project-level control.

Where programs strain

Programs sprawl into dozens of linked sheets. Roll-ups depend on cross-sheet references that break, and the narrative for leadership is still written by hand.

How HorizonView fits

One data model for plans, milestones, RAID and weekly status across every project. AI writes the narrative from that data, and the decks and dashboards update when the data does.

A platform you keep, not one that leaves with the consultants.

What they do well

Proven methods, strong program offices and executive reporting while the firm's team is on site.

Where programs strain

The tool is usually tied to the engagement and the firm's methodology, so access and history can end when the contract does, and it rarely connects to your own systems.

How HorizonView fits

Aberdeen's team implements and supports it, but HorizonView runs in your Microsoft 365, your cloud or a dedicated environment, configured to your methodology. When the engagement ends, the platform and the data stay with you.
